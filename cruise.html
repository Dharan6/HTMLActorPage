Tom Cruise

Tom Cruise is an American actor and producer. He has received various accolades for his work in film, including three Golden Globe Awards and nominations for three Academy Awards. Cruise is known for his roles in films such as "Top Gun," "Mission: Impossible," and "Jerry Maguire." He is also recognized for his dedication to performing his own stunts and action sequences in movies.

Cruise began acting in the early 1980s and made his breakthrough with leading roles in Risky Business (1983) and Top Gun (1986), the latter earning him a reputation as a sex symbol.[1] Critical acclaim came with his roles in the dramas The Color of Money (1986), Rain Man (1988), and Born on the Fourth of July (1989). For his portrayal of Ron Kovic in the latter, he won a Golden Globe Award and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Actor.
